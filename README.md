# ABOUT THIS ANIME
Slam Dunk (stylized in all caps) is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Takehiko Inoue. It was serialized in Shueisha's shōnen manga magazine Weekly Shōnen Jump from October 1990 to June 1996, with the chapters collected into 31 tankōbon volumes. The story follows Hanamichi Sakuragi, a brash and impulsive high school student who joins a basketball team at Shohoku High School, located in the Shōnan area of Japan.

# ABOUT THIS ANIME
Bakugan Battle Brawlers (爆丸バトルブローラーズ, Bakugan Batoru Burōrāzu) is a Japanese-Canadian animated television series produced by TMS Entertainment, Sega Toys, Japan Vistec, Spin Master, and Nelvana in association with Teletoon. The series was directed by Mitsuo Hashimoto and originally aired on TV Tokyo and its affiliates in Japan from April 2007 to March 2008.

# ABOUT THIS ANIME
Dragon Ball (Japanese: ドラゴンボール, Hepburn: Doragon Bōru) is a Japanese media franchise created by Akira Toriyama. The series follows the adventures of protagonist Son Goku from his childhood through adulthood as he trains in martial arts. Along his journey, he makes several friends, becomes a family man, discovers his alien heritage, and battles a wide variety of villains, many of whom, like him, seek the mystical orbs called the Dragon Balls in order to summon a wish-granting dragon.
